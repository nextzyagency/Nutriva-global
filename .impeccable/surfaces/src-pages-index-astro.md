## Scope

Whole marketing site of Nutriva Global (home, four category pages, Nosotros, Contacto). Visitor mode: Persuade. Audience: B2B trade buyers (distributors, importers, retailers). Action: request the wholesale proposal (form posting to formsubmit), WhatsApp, email. Constraint: every statistic, testimonial and certification is placeholder content and is labelled "Datos de ejemplo" on the page.

Quality bar named by the user: Oatly / Rude Health.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard for a healthy-food supplier, executed at Oatly / Rude Health craft: product photography on soft food-coloured grounds, confident grotesque type, and the proposal request working inside the hero. Refuses the old template: eyebrow labels, gradient text, gold dividers, emoji icon-card grids, a loading screen.

OWN-WORLD: White and oat-paper grounds; deep forest ink #14342B for type and dark bands; leaf green #2F7A4E for every action; one food-field tint per line (oat, sage, apricot, sky) holding its photography. Bricolage Grotesque display, Hanken Grotesk text. Images 20px radius, controls pill-shaped, hairline rules, no card shadows.

STORY: The buyer learns what Nutriva supplies, from where and on what terms, browses the four lines, sees how an order works, and requests a proposal.

Right half: hero photograph at 4:5 on the sage field. Below, the four lines as tinted links.
